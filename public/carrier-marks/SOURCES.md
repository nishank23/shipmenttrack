# Carrier marks

These marks identify the carrier associated with a shipment. They do not imply
affiliation or endorsement. The source pages classify the files as public-domain
text logos and note applicable trademarks.

- [MSC](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mediterranean_Shipping_Company_logo.svg): Mediterranean Shipping Company, SVG extracted by Marsupilami.
- [Ocean Network Express](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ocean_Network_Express_logo.svg): sourced from ONE by Commons contributor Banku. See also the [ONE media room](https://www.one-line.com/en/media-room).
- [Evergreen](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Evergreen_Logo.svg): Evergreen Group, SVG by Roland Rytz.

PIL and unknown carriers use a text badge rather than an unverified mark.
